Types of gears
It is like a circular shaped one made
on a cylindrical metal having teeth cut
in parallel to the axis of the cylinder. It
is called as spur gear.

This gear is used to transmit the

a rotary power only between two parallel

shafts. It is capable of transmitting

moderate force and speed. A smaller

gear is called as pinion. Normally in gear

box this type of gear is used for first and

a - reverse gear speed operations.
