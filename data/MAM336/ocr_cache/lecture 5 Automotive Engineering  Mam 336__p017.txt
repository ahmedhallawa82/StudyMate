Automatic gear box
Operating steps:
1. The engine produces rotational power.
2. The clutch connects the engine to the gearbox.
3. When the driver presses the clutch pedal, the engine is disconnected from the transmission.
4. The driver moves the gear lever to select the required gear.
5. The synchronizer matches the rotational speeds of the components before engagement.
6. The driver releases the clutch, and power is transmitted to the wheels.
Advantages: Simple construction, high efficiency, low manufacturing cost, and direct driver control.
Disadvantages: Requires manual operation and can be tiring in heavy traffic.
